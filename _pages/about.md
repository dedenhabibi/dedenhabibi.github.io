Mr Alfathimy is a postgraduate researcher in Politics and International Relations, focusing on outer space politics (astropolitics). He is the recipient of a [Future 100 PhD](https://le.ac.uk/news/2022/february/future-100) studentship from the University of Leicester's [Space Park Leicester](https://www.space-park.co.uk/), under the supervision of Prof [Andrew Futter](https://le.ac.uk/people/andrew-futter) and Dr [Sally Horrocks](https://le.ac.uk/people/sally-horrocks) (University of Leicester), and Dr [Bleddyn Bowen](https://www.durham.ac.uk/staff/bleddyn-e-bowen/) (Durham University). His doctoral project studies Indonesian satellite communication development to better understand space policy in the Global Space Age. He is also an [Associate Fellow](https://le.ac.uk/research/centres/humanity-space/people/associate-fellows) at the [Leverhulme Centre for Humanity and Space (LCHS)](https://le.ac.uk/research/centres/humanity-space).

Mr Alfathimy is currently on study leave from his role as a space policy researcher at the [National Research and Innovation Agency (BRIN)](https://brin.go.id/), Government of Indonesia. He joined the Indonesian space agency ([National Institute of Aeronautics and Space/LAPAN](https://id.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lembaga_Penerbangan_dan_Antariksa_Nasional)) in 2018, which was eventually merged into BRIN in 2021. Since then, he has been working on the international dimensions of Indonesian space policies and programmes by closely informing policymakers, conducting academic research, and advocating for space issues.
